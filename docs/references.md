- **Heinz-Otto Peitgen, Hartmut Jürgens, Dietmar Saupe**, _Chaos and Fractals: New Frontiers of Science_ (2nd ed., 2004).
- **Steven Strogatz**, _Nonlinear Dynamics and Chaos_ (2nd ed., 2014).
